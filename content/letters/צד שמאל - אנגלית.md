# [צד שמאל - אנגלית]

## 🔗 קישורים ומידע ארכיוני

His Excellency,

The High Commissioner for Palestine,

J e r u s a l e m .

Your Excellency,

I hope you will excuse me for applying to your Excellency in the following literary matter.

My father, Joseph Zvi Rimon, who is considered as one of the best Hebrew poets of our time and known as the only religious poet in the Hebrew language, was one of the victims of the riots of May 1921: he was bestially attacked by savage rioters who cut of his genitals. Since that tragic events which aroused a storm of horror and indignation throughout the Jewish world and also throughout the whole cultural world indescribable suffering, both physical and moral, has been the lot of my poor father. Since then he has been drawing his only comfort from his literary work which enabled him to carry on and forget his affliction.

On the occasion of his fiftieth birthday it has been decided to publish all his works comprising a few thousand poems published at various periods in a number of literary periodicals.

In view of the difficulty now existing to get the paper necessary for such publication I appeal to your Excellency kindly to consider this tragic case and to recommend us to the proper authorities so that by way of exception and in consideration of this worthy cause, a licence be granted us for obtaining the quantity of paper required for the publication of the above mentioned collection of poems.

We sincerely hope that our appeal will not be in vain and thank you in anticipation on behalf of my father.

I have the honour to be,

Your Excellency,

Yours faithfully,

DAVID RIMON

41, Zebulun Str. Tel-Aviv.

This is to certify the truth of the above statements and to recommend Mr. Rimon to his Excellency, the High Commissioner.

President of Association of Hebrew Writers Palestine.
